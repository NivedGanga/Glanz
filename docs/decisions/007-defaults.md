# 007: Configurable defaults

Configuration, not product code, defines: 50 free items, 1 branch, 1 staff login, 3 daily digests, 30 monthly request notifications, 20 monthly AI analyses, a 0.45 trigram threshold, 2-point minimum relevance, 5 recommended shops, 2-unit limited-stock threshold, 24-hour ignored window, and a 12-hour digest interval. Default shop quiet hours are 21:00-09:00 district time. Propose verified badge after in-person identity and shop-photo check by admin; no automated verification. All are revisable without schema changes.
